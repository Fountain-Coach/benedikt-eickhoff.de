INT. QUIET ROOM - MORNING

A thought waits beside the window. It has no demand yet,
only a direction.

                     BENEDIKT
          What remains when the image has moved on?

The image is not an answer. It is the place where attention
starts to gather.

2

EXT. OPEN LANDSCAPE - DAY

The scene widens. A memory vector is a line of travel:
people, questions, places, and the small evidence that keeps
them connected.

[[The image is held long enough to be seen.]]

                     BENEDIKT
          Give the thought a place to stand.

The Teatro score marks the move. The text lets the reader
stay with it.

3

INT. SAME ROOM - EVENING

The image returns as a trace, not a repetition. The post can
be short, or it can keep unfolding. New pages are added when
the reading needs them; no meaning is cut to fit a counter.

                     BENEDIKT
          Leave a clear path to the next day.

FADE OUT.
